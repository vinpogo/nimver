refactor: tag releases by package only when several exist

Namespacing keyed on whether packages were declared, so a repository that
named its single package - the way to keep an unrelated sibling manifest
out of versioning - also switched from `v1.5.0` to `root-v1.5.0`. The two
have nothing to do with each other.

Package names now appear in tags, release commits and progress output only
once a workspace holds more than one package, whether they were detected
or declared.
