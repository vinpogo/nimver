fix: re-record change notes after a rebase

post-commit stands down while a rebase runs, because amending there moves
HEAD out from under the sequencer and aborts it. Nothing picked the work up
afterwards, so a reworded commit kept the note recorded for its old message:
retyping `fix:` to `feat:` left bump=patch behind and silently under-bumped
the next release.

A post-rewrite hook now runs once the rebase has finished, with the commits
it rewrote, and re-records each of their notes. The corrections are folded
into HEAD rather than into each rewritten commit, since moving a note back
would mean rewriting history a second time and a release reads the set of
pending notes, not which commit carries them.
